Random Polytope Barcode Simulator With Spatial Resolution

Explore how stochastic excision, cell-cycle timing, post-induction expansion, and motility shape barcode frequencies and spatial clone recovery.

λ is the mean number of excision events per cell over a 24 h barcode-generation period. By default, each starting cell receives a final barcode without dividing during induction. The checkbox enables cell division during that period while barcode excision continues. The spatial field covers a 500 × 500 px domain. Each excision randomly selects two retained tag positions and removes the inclusive segment between them; an empty barcode is recorded as “0”.

How the simulation runs. Without the checkbox, each starting cell receives one barcode at induction by drawing its excision-event count from Poisson(λ), starting from “123456789” and randomly removing an inclusive segment between two distinct positions after each event; a fully excised barcode is “0”. With the checkbox enabled, the starting cells are founders and recombination runs alongside cell division for a fixed 24 h induction period: each cell has a Poisson(λ/24) excision-event count per hour, and daughters inherit their parent’s current barcode before further excisions. Cells are placed uniformly in a 500 × 500 px field. During the chosen 24, 48, or 72 h tracing window, time advances in 1 h steps. Each cell-cycle duration is sampled from a gamma distribution with the selected mean and fixed CV = 0.25; when a cell reaches its cycle duration, it divides with the selected probability. The mother remains and the daughter inherits its barcode and starting-cell origin. A daughter is placed with a radial displacement whose magnitude is |Normal(0, 6 px)|. At each step, every cell also takes independent x/y motility steps with the selected σ, then is pulled 3% toward the centroid of its starting-cell lineage when within 80 px. Positions are bounded to the field. The “After induction” view shows the population at the end of induction (which may include descendants when division is enabled); “After outgrowth” shows surviving and newly generated cells. Frequencies count all simulated cells, and independent-generation counts are distinct starting cells with that barcode. DBSCAN is applied separately to each barcode on all endpoint cells: sentinel barcodes “0” and “123456789” are excluded; barcodes with fewer than five cells remain uncalled. For each other barcode, ε is selected automatically by the elbow of its sorted fourth-nearest-neighbor distances (maximum perpendicular distance to the endpoint chord), then DBSCAN runs with minPts = 4; groups smaller than five remain uncalled as in the MATLAB caller. If the endpoint exceeds 40,000 cells, the spatial scatter and CSV show complete progeny from a reproducibly selected subset of starting-cell lineages; counts, frequencies, origins, and DBSCAN still use the full population. Growth stops at 500,000 active cells, matching the MATLAB function.
A stochastic, browser-side model based on the Polytope fate-mapping framework described by Postrach et al., “Polytope: High-resolution epitope barcoding for in vivo spatial fate-mapping” (bioRxiv, 2024), and the supplied excision and spatial outgrowth MATLAB workflow. Independent origins are tracked by starting-cell identity. DBSCAN clone labels are spatial barcode components, not known ground-truth clones.
